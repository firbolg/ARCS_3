Build: Packaging Your Project for Deposit

Your project folder is already most of the way to deposit-ready:

project/
├── raw_data/          (never modified)
├── processed_data/
├── scripts/
├── outputs/
├── renv.lock
├── README.md
└── .git/
Note⚪ About This — Where Does It Actually Go?
  • GitHub Release — lightweight, no registry account needed, fine for an occasional artifact.
  • Zenodo — mints a citable DOI and guarantees long-term archival. The more appropriate route for published research.

Reflection: Evaluating Reproducibility

As Ethical Practice - Accountability in research - Transparency serves scientific rigor - Levels the scientific playing field - Reliable translation to clinical practice

As Potential Harm - Data re-identification - Informed consent for secondary use of data - Intensification of extractive research - Data access prioritized over data quality

Tip🔍 Learn More — CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance

Read more: CARE Principles

Reproducibility tools don’t automatically make research ethical — they make it visible. What’s visible can still cause harm if stewardship isn’t part of the practice.

What We Did, and Why

What we built: verified AI-generated code against a checklist, packaged a project folder for deposit, and sat with what transparency costs as well as what it gives.

Why it matters: this is where the whole series points — not just “can you reproduce this,” but “should this exist in the form you’re about to share it in.” That question doesn’t have a script. It has you.
